Client-to-firm matching

  • Algorithm-based matching on IP type, industry, geography (optional), budget alignment, readiness score and practice-area specialization
  • Opt-in showcase: clients choose whether partner firms can see them
  • Blind introductions: client privacy is protected until both sides confirm interest
  • Graduated visibility by client tier

Tools for firms

  • Client search with filters (IP type, industry, readiness score)
  • Saved searches and alerts for matching clients
  • Proposal management
  • Secure document exchange and messaging
  • Performance analytics and client feedback

For clients

  • Anonymous browsing of firm profiles
  • Proposal comparison from multiple firms
  • Transparent pricing from firms

Why it helps your practice

  • Clients arrive with a Certificate of Existence, development timeline, prior-art search results and signed NDAs: the materials the Playbook tells them to bring.
  • Consultation answers (IP type, stage, goals, budget, timeline, prior filings, competitors, differentiation, international plans, support needs) help you scope engagements.
  • Clients learn that timestamps are a first layer and that formal filings and counsel complete the protection.

IP Custody is not a law firm. It doesn't give legal advice or refer clients in exchange for fees where that's prohibited. Partner arrangements will be structured to comply with applicable professional-conduct rules. Firms are responsible for their own ethics compliance.
