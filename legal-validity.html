Legal validity

How blockchain timestamps are treated as evidence

An overview of the rules and decisions IP Custody's evidence design is based on. It is general information, not legal advice. Laws differ by jurisdiction and change over time.

Short answer. IP Custody certificates are designed to serve as evidence of a file's existence and creation date, and to support authentication of digital evidence. They are not a patent or a registered copyright. They are meant to be a low-cost, immediate first layer of proof. Whether a record is admitted in a particular case, and how much weight it gets, is always up to the court.

The legal landscape

JurisdictionRule or decisionWhat it means for timestamp evidence
United States: authenticationFederal Rule of Evidence 901Evidence must be shown to be what it claims to be. A matching SHA-256 hash plus a public blockchain record and a custodian record are designed to support that showing.
United States: self-authenticationFRE 902(13) and 902(14)Allow certain machine-generated electronic records, and data copied from devices or files (often identified by hash values), to be authenticated with a certification from a qualified person instead of live testimony, if the rule's notice and certification requirements are met.
United States: case lawUnited States v. Sterlingov (D.D.C. 2024)A federal court admitted blockchain-analysis evidence after a Daubert challenge. The case was about tracing Bitcoin transactions, not timestamping, but it shows federal courts engaging with blockchain-derived evidence.
United States: state statutesVermont, Arizona, Delaware, IllinoisThese states have passed laws that address blockchain records and signatures.
FranceTribunal judiciaire de Marseille, 20 March 2025 (RG 23/00046, AZ Factory v. Valeria Moda)In a fashion copyright infringement case, the court relied on Bitcoin-anchored blockchain timestamp reports (made through a third-party timestamping service and verified by a court bailiff), together with evidence of public disclosure, to establish the claimant's copyright ownership from the recorded dates. EUIPO case summary.
European UnioneIDAS / eIDAS 2Qualified electronic timestamps from qualified trust service providers benefit from a legal presumption of accuracy. Other electronic timestamps may not be denied legal effect just because they are electronic. IP Custody is not a qualified trust service provider.
ChinaHangzhou Internet Court (2018)Accepted blockchain-preserved evidence in a copyright dispute. The decision is still widely cited.
Italy2019 legislationRecognized the legal effect of timestamps on distributed ledgers.

Case and statute descriptions are summaries for general information. Check current law with an attorney in your jurisdiction.

How the certificate is designed to support evidence

  1. Public blockchain anchor. Planned anchoring to Bitcoin via OpenTimestamps, with optional checkpoints on Ethereum, Polygon or Solana.
  2. Custodian record. A plain statement from IP Custody's custodian of what was received and when.
  3. Full evidence package. Certificate, hash-verification instructions, blockchain explorer link, chain-of-custody log and a QR code to verification.
  4. Built to be corroborated. Designed to sit alongside your other evidence (NDAs, emails, version history, disclosures), the same kind of body of evidence the Marseille court relied on.

Important limitations

  • Certificates prove existence and date, not ownership. You still need to show you created the work.
  • Admissibility is always up to the judge, and the weight given depends on the circumstances.
  • They work best combined with other evidence (NDAs, emails, version history, etc.).
  • They don't replace formal copyright registration or patent and trademark filings. They are the first layer.
  • Until a certificate's chain binding is confirmed, it is an internal record, not a blockchain-anchored one.

Related laws referenced across IP Custody materials

17 U.S.C. § 102

Copyright protects original works of authorship fixed in a tangible medium. Protection starts automatically at fixation, and registration unlocks key remedies.

35 U.S.C. §§ 102 / 103

Novelty and non-obviousness requirements for patents. The U.S. is a first-inventor-to-file system, so filing dates matter. Records of conception can still matter, for example in derivation disputes.

DTSA / UTSA

The Defend Trade Secrets Act (18 U.S.C. § 1836 et seq.) and state Uniform Trade Secrets Acts protect information kept secret through reasonable measures.
