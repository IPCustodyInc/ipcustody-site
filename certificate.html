What each field means

  • Content hash: the SHA-256 fingerprint of the exact bytes you submitted. If someone changes one byte, the hash no longer matches.
  • Recorded at: when IP Custody received the file. Once anchored, the blockchain block time provides an independent time reference.
  • Chain binding: UNBOUND means no transaction has been confirmed yet. A certificate only shows a network, transaction ID and block height after a real transaction has been independently observed. We never invent a transaction ID.
  • Evidence state: Claim or Simulated while unbound. Verified only after confirmation with a matching hash.
  • Custodian record: a plain statement of what was received and when. It doesn't decide who the author is or whether the work is novel.

Design standard

Specialized certificate versions

Every version uses the same layout and custodian record, plus sections for the type of asset.

Default

1. Certificate of Existence

Used for every timestamp on every plan. Title "Certificate of Existence," subheading "Blockchain-Timestamped Proof of Creation," a file preview box on the left and all metadata in a clean table on the right.

AI

2. AI Creation Custody Certificate

For AI prompts and outputs and generated art, code or content. Two preview boxes ("Prompt" and "Output") and a Human Authorship Declaration in which the creator states that the prompt was written by a person and the output was generated under their direction, so both are timestamped together as a record of the creative process.

Copyright in AI-assisted work depends on human authorship. See the U.S. Copyright Office's current guidance and ask an attorney.

Inventions

3. Invention Disclosure Certificate

For hardware and software inventions and provisional patent support. Inventor fields (name, date of conception, witnesses), a detailed description box, and a timestamped Invention Disclosure Record that follows good practice for documenting conception and reduction to practice under U.S. patent law (35 U.S.C. §§ 102/103). It can serve as supporting evidence in a future priority contest or provisional application.

Creative works

4. Copyright Protection Certificate

For art, writing, music, code, designs and video. Includes a copyright notice ("© [YEAR] [NAME]. All rights reserved.") and a timestamped record of the date of creation and fixation in a tangible medium (17 U.S.C. § 102).

Registration recommendation: this certificate gives you immediate evidence of the creation date. To be eligible for statutory damages and attorney fees, register the work with the U.S. Copyright Office within 3 months of publication (or before infringement begins).

Trade secrets

5. Trade Secret Vault Certificate

For formulas, algorithms, recipes, business methods and confidential data. A Trade Secret Protection section records the user's declaration that the material is confidential and lists the secrecy measures in place (AES-256 encryption, access restricted to authorized users). The timestamp shows the material existed and was held in confidence as of the recorded time, which is relevant to the "reasonable measures" element of the Defend Trade Secrets Act (18 U.S.C. § 1836 et seq.) and the Uniform Trade Secrets Act.

Export

Court Evidence Package

A one-click export for your attorney that bundles:

  • The relevant certificate(s)
  • A full blockchain verification report and hash-verification instructions
  • The custodian record
  • The chain-of-custody log
  • A QR code and verification URL

Important. Certificates record existence and integrity at a point in time. They don't prove ownership, inventorship, novelty, trade-secret status or infringement, and they don't guarantee any result in court. Whether and how a certificate is admitted, and how much weight it gets, is up to the court. See Legal validity.
