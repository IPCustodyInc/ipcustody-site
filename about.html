About

Democratizing IP protection

IP Custody exists so that anyone with an idea, not just people who can spend thousands on lawyers, can build a credible evidence trail from Day 0.

Our mission

Traditional IP protection is slow and expensive, and most creators wait too long, or never protect their work at all. IP Custody's thesis is simple: protect on Day 0, then file formal registrations for what's worth it.

We're building a secure, tiered IP custody platform that helps individuals, including those with limited financial means, preserve their intellectual property, get guidance and connect with legal professionals when they're ready.

We also hold ourselves to one principle: we keep technical evidence (a file was recorded at a certain time) separate from legal conclusions (ownership, inventorship, novelty, patentability, infringement, enforceability).

Where we are today

  • Early access: the service isn't live. We're collecting early-access sign-ups.
  • Prototype (September 2026): a working first-upload pathway (consent → SHA-256 hash → Certificate of Existence object → evidence record → audit event) with a matching printable certificate template.
  • Blockchain anchoring: designed (Bitcoin via OpenTimestamps), but deliberately not switched on. Certificates stay UNBOUND until a real transaction is confirmed.
  • Free tools on this site: a Prior Art Search launcher and a local Hash & Proof Tool you can use today. They are separate from the IP Custody service, which isn't live.
  • Next: accounts, the SEED tier, the Legal Toolkit and the concierge onboarding flow. See the roadmap.
