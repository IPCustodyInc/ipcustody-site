IP Custody vs. a provisional patent

A timestamp records the creation date and exact file content. A patent gives exclusive rights to stop others making, using or selling your invention. They complement each other: timestamp every idea first, then file patents on the ideas worth the legal spend.

IP Custody vs. copyright registration

A timestamp records the creation date and exact file content. Copyright registration creates a public record, can be prima facie evidence of validity, and unlocks remedies like statutory damages. The smart approach is to timestamp everything and register the high-value finished works.

Key legal note: copyright protection exists automatically once you create and fix a work. Registration isn't required for protection, but it is required for the big remedies (statutory damages, attorney fees) and generally before a federal lawsuit.

IP Custody vs. trademark registration

A timestamp shows your brand assets (logos, names, slogans, packaging mockups) existed in exact form on a date. A federal trademark registration gives you exclusive nationwide rights to use that identifier in commerce. Timestamp everything, then register the marks you're building a business around.

Public recordPrivate (your encrypted vault)Fully public (searchable)

DurationOngoing while subscribed10 years, renewable

Best forProtecting brand assets from day 1Brands actively selling in commerce

In U.S. trademark law, rights generally come from use in commerce and from filing, not from creation alone. Timestamps can help document when assets were created.

IP Custody vs. OriginStamp

OriginStamp is one of the longest-running and most technically respected blockchain timestamping services, founded around 2015 by Prof. Dr. Bela Gipp's team. It pioneered practical Bitcoin + Merkle-tree timestamping and is loved by developers and researchers. IP Custody is being built as a legal-tech layer on the same core technology, designed for creators, inventors, AI users, startups and law firms.

In short: OriginStamp is a technical standard for raw blockchain timestamps. IP Custody is designed to turn timestamps into a complete, easy-to-use IP evidence system with concierge onboarding, tailored certificates and a protection strategy, at a price that works for creators and startups.
