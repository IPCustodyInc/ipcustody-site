Legal Toolkit

Templates & forms for protecting your IP

Included with your subscription: practical starting-point documents for sharing ideas, documenting inventions and clarifying ownership. The full template text is delivered inside the product. This page only lists what's included.

Disclaimer: These templates are provided for informational purposes only and do not constitute legal advice. Consult a qualified attorney before using these documents in any legal matter.

Included in the toolkit

01

Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA)

For exploring a business opportunity where both sides may share confidential technical and business information. Covers purpose, definition of confidential information, obligations, standard exclusions, term, return of materials, no licence, and governing law.

02

Invention Disclosure Form

Creates a dated record of your conception to support patent applications: inventor information, title, description, technical details, known prior art, development timeline (conception, reduction to practice, prior disclosures), commercial potential, attachments and certification.

03

Work-for-Hire Agreement

An independent contractor agreement for services, work-made-for-hire treatment under U.S. copyright law, a backup assignment of rights, compensation, confidentiality and no competing work during the term.

04

IP Assignment Agreement

Transfers rights in described IP from assignor to assignee, including patents, copyrights, trade secrets, trademarks and other IP rights, with further-assurances and consideration clauses.

05

Confidentiality Best Practices Checklist

Checklists for before disclosure, during collaboration, document management, employee/contractor management, digital security and legal protection, including when to timestamp critical documents with IP Custody.

+

Planned additions

Licensing and usage-licence templates, an AI Ownership Declaration for AI-assisted work, and printable invention disclosure forms.
